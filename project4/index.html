I built a predictive model of diamond prices using classical linear modeling in R. Starting with price as the response, I explored carat, cut, and color, assessing significance through ANOVA and lm() outputs. To improve linearity and stabilize variance, I modeled sqrt(price).

I assessed multicollinearity with variance inflation factors (VIF) and found no concerning correlations among predictors. For parsimony and to curb overfitting, I applied AIC-based stepwise selection; the chosen model is: sqrt(price) ~ carat + color + cut.

Coefficient estimates and p-values show carat as the dominant driver of price, color as a meaningful secondary factor, and cut as a smaller but still relevant contributor. Model diagnostics (residual patterns, normality, homoscedasticity) support validity, though some price variation remains unexplained.

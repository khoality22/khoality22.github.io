About Khoa!

As a 4th-year Statistics & Data Science and Media Arts & Design student, Khoa Nguyen, has a passion for making sense of messy data and transforming it into insights, visuals, and usable products. He has a solid foundation in statisitcal modeling and Python/R/SQL. However, he's interested in merging that rigor with digital media— UI/UX research, interaction design, and prototyping— to build interfaces that are both evidence-driven, delightful to use, but also aesthetic.
